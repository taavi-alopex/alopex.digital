RevOps Maturity Assessment

Evaluate your revenue operations readiness

How to Use This Assessment

Check each item that applies to your organization. Be honest — this is for your benefit. Count your checkmarks at the end to see where you stand and identify improvement areas.

1

Data Foundation

  • Single source of truth for customer data
    All teams reference the same CRM/database for customer information
  • Standardized data entry processes
    Clear rules for how contacts, deals, and activities are logged
  • Regular data hygiene practices
    Scheduled cleanup of duplicates, outdated records, and incomplete entries
  • Clear data ownership
    Defined responsibility for data quality and maintenance
  • Lead source tracking
    Every lead's origin is captured and attributed correctly
2

Process & Workflow

  • Documented sales process
    Clear stages from lead to close with defined criteria for each
  • Lead handoff process defined
    Marketing to sales handoff is clear with SLAs
  • Follow-up sequences automated
    No leads fall through cracks due to manual follow-up failures
  • Onboarding process standardized
    New customer experience is consistent and documented
  • Renewal/upsell workflows exist
    Proactive processes for customer expansion and retention
3

Technology Stack

  • CRM is actively used by all revenue teams
    Sales, marketing, and CS all work from the same system
  • Marketing automation connected to CRM
    Email, forms, and campaigns sync automatically
  • No manual data transfer between tools
    Integrations handle data flow automatically
  • Communication tools integrated
    Email, calls, and meetings logged automatically
  • Document/proposal tools connected
    Quotes and contracts sync with deals
4

Reporting & Insights

  • Revenue dashboard exists and is current
    Real-time visibility into pipeline and performance
  • Conversion rates tracked at each stage
    Know exactly where deals get stuck or lost
  • Customer acquisition cost (CAC) known
    Can calculate cost to acquire by channel
  • Customer lifetime value (LTV) calculated
    Understand long-term customer worth
  • Regular reporting rhythm established
    Weekly/monthly reviews with actionable insights
