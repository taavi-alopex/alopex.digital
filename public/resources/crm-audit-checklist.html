Contact Data Quality

  • Merge duplicate contacts High
    Run duplicate detection and merge/delete. Check by email, phone, and name variations.
  • Remove bounced/invalid emails High
    Export all contacts with hard bounces. Archive or delete to protect sender reputation.
  • Archive inactive contacts (12+ months)
    Contacts with no opens, clicks, or activity for over a year. Move to archive list.
  • Fill missing required fields
    Run report on contacts missing company, phone, or other critical fields.
  • Standardize field formats
    Phone numbers, addresses, company names — ensure consistent formatting.
💰

Deals & Pipeline

  • Close stale deals High
    Deals with no activity for 60+ days. Mark as lost or follow up immediately.
  • Verify deal amounts Medium
    Check that deal values are realistic and updated after negotiations.
  • Update close dates
    Past-due close dates destroy forecast accuracy. Update or close.
  • Ensure owner assignment
    Every deal should have an owner. Unassigned = forgotten.
🏢

Companies & Accounts

  • Merge duplicate companies
    Same company, different spellings (Inc vs Inc. vs Incorporated).
  • Link orphan contacts to companies
    Contacts without company associations miss important context.
  • Update company information
    Industry, size, revenue — enrich from LinkedIn or data providers.
⚙️

System & Process

  • Review custom fields
    Delete unused fields. Consolidate similar ones. Document what each means.
  • Audit pipeline stages
    Are stages clearly defined? Do they match your actual sales process?
  • Check automation triggers
    Review workflows. Disable broken ones. Update outdated triggers.
  • Verify integrations
    Are all connected apps still syncing correctly? Check error logs.
