Churn Prevention Checklist

Identify risks early. Act before it's too late.

Customer Health Indicators
Login drop >50% from baseline
Open support ticket >7 days
Key feature unused 30+ days
Champion left company
Payment failure (any)
No QBR scheduled this quarter
👁

Proactive Monitoring

  • Set up usage tracking alerts
    Get notified when customer activity drops below threshold
    Automate: Daily usage report → Alert if <50% of baseline
  • Monitor support ticket sentiment
    Flag frustrated customers before they escalate
    Automate: Negative sentiment → Escalate to CSM
  • Track champion job changes
    Know when your main contact leaves or changes roles
    Automate: LinkedIn integration → Alert on job change
  • Payment failure recovery
    Don't let involuntary churn happen
    Automate: Failed charge → Email sequence + CSM alert
🤝

Engagement Rituals

  • Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)
    Review ROI, set goals, identify expansion opportunities
    Automate: QBR calendar invites 2 weeks before quarter end
  • New feature announcements
    Keep customers excited about product improvements
    Automate: Feature release → Personalized email to relevant customers
  • Success milestone celebrations
    Acknowledge when customers hit key achievements
    Automate: 100th lead captured → Congratulations email
  • Renewal check-in (90 days out)
    Start renewal conversation early
    Automate: 90 days before renewal → CSM task + customer email
At-Risk Customer Save Playbook
Acknowledge quickly (within 24 hours)
Personal outreach from CSM. "I noticed [specific issue]. Let's talk."
Listen and diagnose
Schedule call. Ask open questions. Understand root cause before proposing solutions.
Propose specific solution
Training session, feature walkthrough, process adjustment — match solution to problem.
Follow up relentlessly
Check in at 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. Ensure issue is truly resolved.
Document and learn
Log the issue and resolution. Look for patterns to prevent for other customers.
