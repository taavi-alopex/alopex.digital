Growth Stage Playbook

What to focus on at each revenue milestone

Stage 1

Foundation

€0 - €10K MRR

Primary Focus

  • Find product-market fit
  • Define ideal customer profile
  • Establish repeatable sales process
  • Set up basic CRM tracking

Tools to Implement

  • Simple CRM (HubSpot Free, Pipedrive)
  • Basic email tool (Mailchimp, ConvertKit)
  • Calendar booking (Calendly)
  • Analytics (Google Analytics)

Traction

€10K - €50K MRR

Primary Focus

Tools to Add

  • Marketing automation platform
  • Lead scoring system
  • Customer success tools
