Year-End Campaign Ideas
"Use It or Lose It" Budget Campaign
Target finance/ops contacts with messaging about end-of-year budget spending. Offer early-bird pricing for Jan starts.
Annual Plan Discount
Offer 2 months free for annual commitments made before Dec 31. Creates urgency and improves cash flow.
Renewal + Expansion Bundle
For renewals, offer upgrade discount if they commit before EOY. Captures expansion revenue.
"Start Fresh in 2027" Onboarding
Promise dedicated Jan onboarding slots for deals closed in Dec. Reduces friction.
